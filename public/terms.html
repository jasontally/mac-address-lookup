The service, and what it is not

The site resolves a MAC address or an OUI prefix to the organization that holds the matching block in the public IEEE Registration Authority registries. That is the whole service. It is a reference lookup for people who work with networks.

It is not an investigative tool. It does not identify a person, a device owner, a subscriber, or a user. It does not tell you who is behind an address on a network, whether a device is yours, or whether anyone is doing anything unlawful. A prefix resolves to a registered organization; the IEEE registries say nothing about the individuals who use hardware from that organization, and you must not treat a result as evidence about any person.
